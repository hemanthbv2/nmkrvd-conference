About the Conference

Bridging Research & Real-World Innovation

A multidisciplinary platform bringing together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to share innovative ideas and practical solutions.

🔬

Conference Theme

Intelligent Computing, Quantum Technologies, and Sustainable Innovations highlights the role of emerging technologies in creating a smarter, more sustainable future. It brings together diverse disciplines — Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Quantum Computing, and intelligent systems — to bridge the gap between research and real-world applications. The theme also emphasizes sustainable technologies, including green computing, energy-efficient data centers, renewable energy, and environmentally responsible digital transformation.

🏛️

About NMKRV College

NMKRV College, Bengaluru, is one of Karnataka's premier institutions of higher education, established in 1973 under the aegis of the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST). Affiliated with Bengaluru University and accredited by NAAC, the college is committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development. The Department of Computer Science and Computer Applications is at the forefront of research and technology education.

Manuscript Submission Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to submit your research paper for review.

📝 Submission Requirements

  • Submit a full paper in A4 format using IEEE templates
  • All submissions must be in English only
  • Submit manuscripts electronically through Microsoft CMT
  • All papers will undergo double-blind peer review
  • Follow IEEE formatting and citation standards

📚 Review & Publication

All submitted papers will pass through a rigorous double-blind peer review process. Accepted papers must meet IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements for inclusion.

  • Double-blind peer review process
  • Accepted papers submitted to IEEE Xplore
  • Camera-ready version due by July 7, 2027
  • Authors must register before final submission
