International Conference on Intelligent Computing, Quantum Technologies, and Sustainable Innovations (ICIQSI 2027) on 15 & 16th September -2027

Shaping the Future of Intelligent Computing and Sustainability

Intelligent Computing, Quantum Technologies, and Sustainable Innovations is a multidisciplinary theme that highlights the role of emerging technologies in creating a smarter, more sustainable future. It brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to share innovative ideas and practical solutions in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Quantum Computing, and intelligent systems. The theme also emphasizes sustainable technologies, including green computing, energy-efficient data centers, renewable energy, and environmentally responsible digital transformation. By encouraging collaboration across diverse disciplines, it aims to bridge the gap between research and real-world applications. Ultimately, this theme seeks to inspire innovations that improve quality of life, support sustainable development, and address the technological challenges of tomorrow.

About NMKRV

NMKRV College , Bengaluru, is one of Karnataka's premier institutions of higher education, established in 1973 under the aegis of the Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST). Affiliated with Bengaluru University and accredited by NAAC, the college is committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development.

Conference Themes

Conference tracks, but not limited to:

  1. AI, Computer Vision, NLP, and Sustainable Computing Systems
  2. Next-Generation Information and Quantum Computing Technologies
  3. Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Blockchain Technologies

Key areas of Track 1:

This track explores the latest advancements in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and sustainable computing. It encourages innovative research that develops smart, efficient, and environmentally responsible solutions for real-world challenges.

    • Computer Vision
    • Image Processing
    • NLP and Large Language Models
    • Generative AI
    • Explainable AI
    • Green AI
    • AI in Indian Knowledge System
    • Sustainable Computing
    • Energy-Efficient Computing
    • Green Data Centers
    • AI for Smart Energy Management
    • Carbon-Aware Computing
    • Sustainable Cloud and Edge Computing

Key areas of Track 2:

This track explores the latest developments in information systems and quantum computing. The track encourages innovative research that advances faster, smarter, and more secure technologies for the future of digital transformation.

  • Quantum Information Processing
  • Quantum Communication
  • Quantum Cryptography
  • Quantum Machine Learning (QML)
  • Quantum Optimization

Key areas of Track 3:

This track focuses on the latest advances in cloud computing, cybersecurity, and blockchain. It encourages research that builds secure, reliable, and scalable digital solutions for today's connected world

    • Internet of Things (IoT)
    • Industrial IoT
    • Edge and Fog Computing
    • Digital Twins
    • Smart Manufacturing
    • Autonomous Systems
    • AI Security
    • Cybersecurity
    • Blockchain Technologies
    • Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning
    • Quantum-Safe Cryptography
