Submission & Publication

Follow these guidelines to submit your research paper for review.

📝 Submission Requirements

  • Submit a full paper in A4 format using IEEE templates
  • All submissions must be in English only
  • Submit manuscripts electronically through Microsoft CMT
  • All papers will undergo double-blind peer review
  • Follow IEEE formatting and citation standards
📥 Download IEEE Template →

📚 Review & Publication

All submitted papers will pass through a rigorous double-blind peer review process. Accepted papers must meet IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements for inclusion.

  • Double-blind peer review process
  • Accepted papers submitted to IEEE Xplore
  • Camera-ready version due by July 7, 2027
  • Authors must register before final submission
